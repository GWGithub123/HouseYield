About Us

Making Real Estate
Work for Everyone

HouseYield was founded on a simple idea: owning and investing in rental properties shouldn't require juggling spreadsheets, phone calls, and guesswork. We built a single platform where owners, investors, tenants, and contractors can manage, analyze, and grow together.

Our Mission

We believe every property owner and real estate investor — whether you manage one unit or one hundred — deserves professional-grade tools. HouseYield empowers you to find great investments, plan value-add renovations, manage day-to-day operations, track market trends, and build long-term wealth.

Why HouseYield?

Most real estate tools force you to choose: property management OR investment analysis. HouseYield combines both in one platform, designed from the ground up for independent landlords, hands-on investors, and growing portfolios — powerful tools without the complexity or enterprise price tags.

What We Offer

Property Management

Manage your entire portfolio from one dashboard — track occupancy, collect rent, handle maintenance requests, screen tenants, create leases, and monitor financial performance at a glance.

Investment Analysis

Search for new rental investment opportunities, evaluate cap rates and cash-on-cash returns, track housing market trends and interest rates, and build a data-driven acquisition strategy.

Renovation Planning

Plan value-add renovations with cost estimates, projected value increases, and ROI rankings. See which improvements deliver the best returns before you spend a dollar.

Wealth & Retirement Tracking

Track your total net worth across real estate, stocks, and other assets. Project future income, model retirement scenarios, and watch your wealth grow over time.

For Tenants

Pay rent online, submit and track maintenance requests with photos, view your lease, and communicate directly with your landlord — all through a clean, easy-to-use interface.

For Contractors

Browse renovation projects on the marketplace, submit competitive bids, access property details, update work status in real time, and coordinate directly with property managers.
